New here? How Bridge Hub works

A private shelf and message board for your people: family, friends or any group. Free, and works in any phone browser.

Get started

  1. Tap Create account. Pick a username and password. No email needed, and no password reset, so save it.
  2. Pick your user name (like "Sam") and confirm 3 times. The first user can choose to be the Manager.
  3. Save your recovery code and add the app to your home screen.
  4. Share the username and password with the people you want in, whether that's family, friends or coworkers. Everyone on that login shares one shelf and board, each under their own user name.
  5. Already have a login? Tap Sign in, then pick your name (or recover it with your code).

What it does

  • Shelf. Drop text, links or files on one phone and grab them on another. Clears itself after 7 days.
  • Message board. Post notes tagged Note, Idea, Question, Decision or Urgent, with an optional due date.
  • Private posts. Under "Who sees it," turn off anyone to send only to the users you pick.
  • Threads. Reply to a post or to a specific reply. Posts can be marked resolved.
  • Tag people. Tap a user's name to call them out; the post gets highlighted for them.
  • Files and photos. Attach up to 10 MB per file. The Files tab lists everything shared.
  • Projects. Anyone can start a separate board and choose which users can see it.
  • NEW badges and live updates. Unread posts stay marked until you open them.
  • Activity feed. See the latest posts and replies at a glance.
  • Locked user names. Each phone keeps one name, so you always know who posted. (A user is any phone or device labeled with that person's name.)

If you're the Manager

  • Manage users. Add, remove or unlock users, or hand off the manager role.
  • Moderate. Pin, resolve or delete any post or reply.
  • Read tracking. See who read each post, who was on but skipped it, and who opened files.
  • Manager mode switch. Turn the tools off to see the app like everyone else.

Private by design: each login only sees its own shelf and boards, and no email or phone number is collected. Don't post passwords or anything highly sensitive.
